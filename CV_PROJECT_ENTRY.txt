E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics | SQL, Python, R
- Built an end-to-end analytics project using SQL, Python and R to analyse 8,000+ e-commerce transactions.
- Used SQL joins, aggregations and business queries to evaluate revenue, profit, products, customers and regions.
- Used Python/Pandas for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, KPI calculations and RFM customer segmentation.
- Used R for correlation analysis and regression modelling to investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Produced business-focused visualisations and recommendations from analytical findings.
