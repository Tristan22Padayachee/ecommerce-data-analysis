# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **e-commerce data analytics project** designed to analyse sales performance, customer behaviour and business trends using **SQL, Python and R**.

The project demonstrates how data analysts can transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights that can support decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyse overall e-commerce sales performance
* Identify the best-performing products and categories
* Analyse customer purchasing behaviour
* Identify sales trends over time
* Calculate important business metrics
* Use SQL to perform structured data analysis
* Use Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis
* Use R for statistical analysis and visualisation
* Develop actionable business recommendations

---

## 🏢 Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. Which products generate the most revenue?
3. Which product categories perform best?
4. What are the monthly sales trends?
5. Who are the highest-value customers?
6. What is the average order value?
7. Which products have the highest demand?
8. Are there noticeable patterns in customer purchasing behaviour?
9. What areas of the business could be improved?

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, potential business recommendations include:

### 1. Focus on high-performing products

Identify products generating the highest revenue and ensure sufficient stock availability.

### 2. Improve underperforming categories

Investigate low-performing categories and consider pricing, promotions, product selection and marketing strategies.

### 3. Target high-value customers

Develop loyalty programmes and personalised offers for customers with high purchase frequency or high lifetime value.

### 4. Analyse seasonal trends

Use historical sales patterns to improve inventory planning and promotional campaigns.

### 5. Use data-driven decision making

Continue monitoring sales and customer KPIs to identify changes in business performance.
